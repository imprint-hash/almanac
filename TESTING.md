# How Almanac is tested

Almanac gives people odds about money. A wrong percentage, an invented figure or a clock that lies is not a cosmetic bug here, so every change is tested by [TestSprite](https://www.testsprite.com) before it can reach the live site.

## What runs, and when

- **46 browser tests** generated by TestSprite's agents from the live app and from [a product description](docs/testsprite-prd.md) that says what must be true and what must never happen.
- They run on **every pull request**, against that pull request's own Vercel preview, through the TestSprite GitHub App. The result is posted on the pull request.
- The suite covers the odds board, a token's reading, the market switch, search, the report card, the ask box, the New York clock, the MCP card, light and dark mode, and **replay** at the moments that matter: before the bell, after it, on a weekend, on a market holiday, and a refused future time.

## What the tests check that a click-through would not

- **The numbers.** Every percentage between 0 and 100, "stands" and "undone" adding up, counts matching their percentages.
- **The AI's honesty.** An answer from the model may only use figures the desk gave it. The figures are shown under every answer so a reader can check them too.
- **The clock.** With replay (`?at=`), the page can be tested at 3 a.m., at the bell, on a holiday, not only at whatever time the test happens to run.

Each line is a real run. "Caught" means TestSprite found something wrong that was then fixed in the next commit. Nothing here was planted.
